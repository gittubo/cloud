# Two-Week Validation Kit

Goal: test three ideas for ₦0 in 14 days, then commit to the winner.

| Idea | Success signal by Day 14 |
|---|---|
| Power tracker | Neighbours keep posting light status without you reminding them |
| Church app | 2+ pastors say "I'd pay" or ask "when can I use it?" |
| Diaspora care | 10+ replies, some saying they'd pay |

Keep a simple note (phone Notes app is fine) for each idea: who you spoke to, what they said **in their own words**, and anything surprising. Bring it back here on Day 14.

---

## 1. Power tracker — WhatsApp group

**Group name:** `[Your Street/Estate] Light Watch ⚡`

**Invite message (send to neighbours one by one, not as a broadcast):**

> Good evening! I'm starting a small WhatsApp group for our street so we always know when light comes or goes — no more guessing or calling around. Just post "light don come" or "light don go" when it happens. Want me to add you?

**Pinned group description:**

> ⚡ Light Watch — rules
> 1. When light comes, post: ✅ LIGHT ON
> 2. When light goes, post: ❌ LIGHT OFF
> 3. That's it. No forwards, no ads, no politics.
> Together we'll learn our area's light pattern.

**Your job:**
- Days 1–3: post updates yourself every time light changes so people see how it works.
- Days 4–14: **stop posting first.** Watch whether others keep it going. This is the real test.
- Day 7: ask the group: *"Would it help if an app showed you when light usually comes in our area, and how much you spend on fuel vs. solar?"* Note the replies.

**Track daily:** number of members, number of updates posted by people other than you.

## 2. Church app — pastor conversations

**How to ask for the meeting:**

> Good day Pastor. I'm a young entrepreneur working on a tool to help churches manage members, attendance and announcements more easily. I'm not selling anything — I just want to learn how you run things today. Could I have 15 minutes of your time this week?

**Questions (listen more than you talk; don't pitch until the end):**

1. How many members do you have? How many attend on a normal Sunday?
2. How do you keep track of members today — book, Excel, WhatsApp, memory?
3. How do you know when a member has stopped coming?
4. How do you send announcements? How many WhatsApp groups does the church run?
5. How are offerings and tithes recorded? Who handles it?
6. Are sermons recorded? Do you share them afterwards?
7. What is the most stressful admin part of running the church?
8. Have you ever paid for any software or app for the church? Which one, and why did you stop or continue?
9. *(Only now, briefly describe the idea)* If a simple tool did [the thing they complained about most], would that be useful? What would you pay monthly for it?
10. Do you know other pastors I could talk to?

**Watch for:** strong pain ("this thing is killing me"), money already being spent, and requests to see it. Polite interest ("it's a nice idea") does **not** count.

## 3. Diaspora care — online groups

**Where to look:** Facebook groups like "Nigerians in UK/US/Canada", Nigerian professional groups on LinkedIn, Nairaland diaspora section, X (search "parents back home Nigeria").

Read each group's rules first. If posting is not allowed, message members directly instead.

**Group post:**

> Question for Nigerians abroad 🇳🇬
> Do you worry about your parents' health back home — whether they're taking their medication, seeing the doctor, or that the money you send is going where it should?
> I'm based in Nigeria and exploring a way to make this easier. I'd love to ask 5 quick questions (5 minutes, by chat or call). Comment or DM me. Thank you!

**Questions:**

1. Where are your parents, and how old are they?
2. How do you currently keep up with their health?
3. What is your biggest worry about them right now?
4. How much do you send home monthly, and roughly how much goes to health?
5. Have you ever paid for anything to help — a caregiver, a nurse visit, a service? How did it go?
6. *(Then describe briefly)* If a service checked on them daily in their own language, showed you how they're doing, and let you pay their pharmacy or clinic directly — would you pay ~$15/month? Why or why not?
